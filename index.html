Discover Chandrakota: A Serene Village

Welcome to the official website of Chandrakota, a serene and beautiful village nestled in the Katwa PS area of Purba Bardhaman District, West Bengal, India. Our village spans an area of approximately 1.2 square kilometers and is a vibrant community with a rich local culture.

Chandrakota is uniquely blessed by the presence of the small but significant Sonajan River, flowing gracefully along the eastern side of our village, enhancing its natural beauty and providing a tranquil environment.

Sonajan River flowing by Chandrakota.

Geographic & Community Snapshot

Chandrakota is located at latitude 23.5984284° N and longitude 88.0987751° E, PIN: 713150.

With an approximate population of 2000+, Chandrakota is a close-knit community. The village is proud to host two major mosques and a long-standing primary school.

Demographics and Community Life

Chandrakota is a village under Katwa PS, Purba Bardhaman District, West Bengal, PIN 713150. Population ~2000.

The village has a 100% Islamic population, reflecting strong adherence to Islamic traditions and values.

Religious Institutions

Masjid Baitul Mokkaram: A two-floor mosque with multiple minarets.

📰 Chandrakota Village News

Stay updated with the latest happenings from our community.

🌟 Annual Cultural Meet 2026

📅 23 August 2026 • by Md Hosain Sk

The Chandrakota community is gearing up for its annual cultural meet on 15 December 2026. All residents are invited to participate in music, poetry, and traditional performances. Let's celebrate our heritage together!

📚 New Maktab Classes for Adults

📅 20 August 2026 • by Imam R. Ali

To promote lifelong learning, the village Maktabs have started evening classes for adults. The program focuses on Quranic recitation, basic Arabic, and Islamic values. Registration is open at the central mosque.

🚰 Clean Water Initiative Launched

📅 15 August 2026 • by Village Panchayat

A new water purification system has been installed near the main mosque, providing safe drinking water to all residents. The project was funded by community contributions and local government support.

🏏 Chandrakota Cricket Team Wins Local Cup

📅 10 August 2026 • by Sports Club

Our village cricket team emerged victorious in the Katwa Block Inter-Village Tournament. The team was felicitated by the village elders and residents. Congratulations to all the players!
